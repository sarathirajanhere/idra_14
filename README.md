🍔 Food Delivery Data Visualization Portfolio

Day 14 — Data Science Program

    A Matplotlib & Seaborn visualization portfolio exploring food delivery business performance through time trends, category comparisons, distributions and relationships.

🎯 Objectives

1.Analyze orders and revenue over time

2.Compare cities and cuisines

3.Explore marketing spend vs revenue

4.Study delivery time and customer ratings

5.Investigate weather and order-channel performance

6.Communicate insights through appropriate visualizations

📊 Visualizations

📈 Line Plot — Orders & Revenue over time

📊 Bar Chart — Revenue by City

🔵 Scatter Plot — Marketing Spend vs Revenue

📉 Histogram — Delivery Time

📦 Box Plot — Ratings by Weather

🎻 Violin Plot — Ratings by Cuisine

🧾 Count Plot — Order Channels

🍜 Bar Chart — Orders by Cuisine

🌦️ Weather performance analysis

🔥 Correlation Heatmap

Each chart includes a title, axis labels, formatting and a brief interpretation.

📌 Key Takeaways

Business performance changes over time.

City and cuisine demand are not evenly distributed.

Marketing investment can be evaluated against revenue.

Delivery time is an important operational metric.

Weather and order channels provide useful performance segments.
